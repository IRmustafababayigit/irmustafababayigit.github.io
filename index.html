Reproducible Instability-Axis Reconstruction and Regime-Dependent RAR Analysis in Disk Galaxies
Combined reproducible empirical investigation of regime-dependent behavior in the Radial Acceleration Relation (RAR), instability-axis reconstruction, dwarf-galaxy dynamical structure, residual-flow organization, and galaxy-regime transitions.

The work focuses on transparent and reproducible analysis of galactic dynamics using statistical robustness tests, cross-validation, regime diagnostics, and empirical dynamical-system structure, without introducing speculative new physics claims.
